RESEARCH / 01

02 Research

What's core problem?

Conventional weed control often relies on manual labour or herbicides. We explored two emerging herbicide-free approaches and the trade-offs each introduces.

Mechanical weeding

Reliable cultivation between crop rows, but physical tools disturb the soil and can be difficult to use close to crops.

Garford InRow →

Laser weeding

Highly targeted thermal treatment without mechanical contact, but commercial systems can be expensive and technically complex.

Carbon Robotics LaserWeeder G2 →

OUR RESEARCH QUESTION

To analyze modern spot-weeding methods and confirm the effectiveness of the laser approach by developing a hybrid WeedZap prototype that combines laser and mechanical weed removal using computer vision.

We reviewed Garford InRow and Carbon Robotics systems, alongside made research on diode-laser weed treatment.

04 Computer vision

Computer vision for weed detection

We trained a custom YOLOv8 weed-detection model using images collected. The dataset was annotated for weed recognition and used to evaluate the model's ability to distinguish weeds against natural backgrounds.

SAFE-ZONE

We implemented a morphological mask-expansion method (5 mm) to create a protective buffer around crop regions and reduce the risk of targeting cultivated plants.

Development status: During the documented experimental phase, targeting was performed manually using a live video feed and reference frame. Full real-time integration of CV coordinates with the motion platform remained a future development step.

05 Experiments

Testing the laser treatment

We tested short laser exposure directed at the base of the weed stem, then documented visible changes over five days. The main recorded treatment condition was 7 W for 1–2 seconds.

Observed outcome

In the documented test series, 7W * (1~2)s exposure resulted in wilting in 80% of cases (N = 10). The photo observations showed progressive tissue darkening, loss of turgor and wilting over the following days.

These are prototype-scale experimental results, not a claim of proven field-wide performance. Further trials and dose calibration are needed.
